# Suno Format: «Еще не раз вы вспомните меня»
# Николай Гумилёв

## STYLE-BLOCK

90s Russian tragic pop-ballad, melancholic synthpop, 120 BPM, D-minor.
Male lead vocal with warm tenor and emotional delivery, supported by soft male harmonies in chorus.
Mood: Bittersweet, nostalgic, dramatic — a farewell confession wrapped in a danceable beat.
Instrumentation: Punchy 909 kick, warm analog bass, bright supersaw pad, soft orchestral strings, clean electric guitar arpeggios in the bridge, gated reverb snare in intro, vocoder spoken hook.
Structure: 0:00 Intro with vocoder phrase and gated snare → 0:18 Verse 1 (half-time feel) → 0:48 Chorus with full beat and string swell → 1:18 Vocal interlude with wordless ad-lib → 1:30 Verse 2 (reprise of hook material) → 2:00 Chorus → 2:30 Vocal interlude → 2:42 Bridge (spoken-shout build-up) → 3:12 Final Chorus with stadium reverb → 3:42 Outro fade with vocal chops and echoing phrase.
Mastering: Modern clean production, −14 LUFS, wide stereo, female vocal upfront, light tape saturation, soft compression.
